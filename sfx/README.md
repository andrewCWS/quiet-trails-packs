# Sound effects

Drop audio files in this folder, named exactly as the key in the table below,
any common format (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`). `narrate.py` will look them up by
name, so `kookaburra.mp3` matches a `(sfx: kookaburra)` marker in a script.

Don't worry about trimming, levels or fades — the generator already trims,
normalises and fades every clip it joins, and effects go through the same
treatment. Mono or stereo, any sample rate. Just get the right sound.

**Record `CREDITS.md` as you go.** This repo is public and most Creative
Commons licences require attribution. A file with no credit line is a file we
have to delete later.

## Two kinds

**Punctuation** — one sound at a moment, at full attention, in the gap between
sentences. The kookaburra laughing. A car door.

**Beds** — ambience running quietly underneath a passage, roughly 18 dB down,
until it's told to stop. Campfire, river, road noise, cicadas. These want to be
long (30 s+) and loopable; everything else can be a second or two.

## Record these yourself at Ganguddy

Better than anything in a library, and no licence to track. In rough order of
value:

`dawn-still` · `river-lap` · `wind-casuarina` · `campfire` · `leaf-litter`
· `footsteps-leaves` · `gravel-tyres` · `cicadas` · `frogs-night`

Phone voice memo is fine. Long takes, no talking, and hold still — 30 seconds
of nobody moving is worth more than five minutes of a good try.
