Quiet Trails · Ganguddy

Stories for the drive

Thirty-seven short stories about the place we're going — the green river, the orange rocks, the bird in the leaves who can copy any sound she's heard. About five minutes each, near enough three hours all up. Every one ends with something to go and find.

https://andrewcws.github.io/quiet-trails-packs/audio/index.xml

On an iPhone

  1. Open Podcasts — the purple app that came with the phone.
  2. Tap Library along the bottom.
  3. Tap the ••• button in the top right corner.
  4. Choose Add a Show by URL…
  5. Paste the address above and tap Subscribe.

No account, no sign-in, nothing to pay. It just turns up in your library like any other show.

On an Android

  1. Install AntennaPod — it's free and has no ads.
  2. Tap the + button.
  3. Choose Add Podcast by RSS address.
  4. Paste the address and subscribe.

Pocket Casts works too — paste the address into its search box and it'll find the feed.

Before you leave

Download every episode while you're still on wi-fi. There's no phone reception at Ganguddy, and podcast apps stream by default — so an episode you haven't downloaded is an episode that won't play at the park gate.

  1. Open the show and tap the download arrow on each episode. It's 37 taps and about 180 MB — roughly one video of a kid jumping off a rock.
  2. Wait until no episode still shows a little cloud icon.
  3. Turn on Aeroplane Mode and play episode 1 for thirty seconds. If it plays, you're set.

If the episodes are listed newest-first, pull down on the show page to refresh — they're meant to run 1 to 37 in order.
