Quiet Trails · Ganguddy

Stories for the drive

Thirty-seven short stories about the place we’re going — the green river, the orange rocks, the bird in the leaves who can copy any sound she’s heard. About five minutes each. Every one ends with something to go and find.

https://andrewcws.github.io/quiet-trails-packs/audio/index.xml

Download the episodes on wi-fi before you leave. There’s no reception at Ganguddy, and podcast apps stream by default.

How to add it to your phone

iPhone — Podcasts → Library → ••• top right → Add a Show by URL → paste → Subscribe

Android — AntennaPod → + → Add Podcast by RSS address → paste

Then — open the show and tap the download arrow on each episode. About 180 MB all up.
